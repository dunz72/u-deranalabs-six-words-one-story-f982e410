An entry for the [Six Words, One Story](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410) bounty on make.fun: one complete story told in exactly six words.

## The story

> She kept his letters. Burned mine.

Six words, one marriage's ending: she treasured another man's letters and destroyed the narrator's. Who he was, what happened between them, and what comes next are left for the reader to fill in.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (stdlib only) validates the story is exactly six words and 1–60 characters, then writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `/output/entry.txt` when that variable is unset).
